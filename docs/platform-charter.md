# Platform charter

## Purpose

Niytrix is intended to determine whether tightly controlled, deterministic
trading strategies can produce a durable after-cost edge without endangering the
owner's salary, long-term wealth, family assets, health, or primary employment.

The platform is not assumed to be profitable. Its first product is trustworthy
evidence: reproducible research, explainable decisions, bounded experiments, and
records strong enough to support a decision to scale, redesign, hold, or stop.

## Executive direction

- Retain the wealth plan as the governing constitution.
- Develop Swing V1 as the only primary strategy candidate.
- Keep the 15-minute NSE intraday strategy in a ring-fenced laboratory until it
  independently passes its gates.
- Share deterministic platform capabilities where useful, while keeping each
  strategy independently falsifiable.
- Prefer small, reversible, observable decisions over premature complexity.

## Prime directive

> Salary protects the base. Evidence—not deposits, confidence, or AI—releases
> trading capital.

## Product principles

1. **Protect the base.** Emergency reserves, family assets, long-term investments,
   and unrelated business funds do not repair trading losses.
2. **Evidence before capital.** Each stage has predeclared statistical,
   operational, and behavioural gates.
3. **Deterministic execution.** The same versioned rules should drive tests,
   replay, shadow operation, and live decisions.
4. **Broker truth.** Broker order, fill, and position books are authoritative when
   local state differs.
5. **Human accountability.** Automation reduces monitoring; it does not remove
   ownership, reconciliation, or approval.
6. **AI is advisory.** AI may summarize evidence and draft hypotheses, but it may
   not authorize trades, risk, configuration, or deployment.
7. **Life fit is a gate.** The system must fit around paid work and a sustainable
   weekly operating budget.

## Current scope

The planning scope covers NSE cash-equity swing trading, a cash-equity intraday
research strategy, market-data handling, deterministic rules, risk controls,
broker execution, journaling, reconciliation, evidence production, and human
operations.

Options, strategy blending, autonomous AI decisions, a custom multi-screen UI,
microservices, and infrastructure built for hypothetical scale are outside the
initial scope.

## Definition of success

Success is a tested platform and a defensible decision. A profitable strategy is
one possible outcome; clean evidence that supports stopping is also valuable. No
live date, return, or capital scale is guaranteed.
