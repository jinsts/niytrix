The executive answer

The two reports are complementary, but they operate at different levels: one governs wealth and capital allocation; the other provides a lean implementation for a specific intraday system.

Recommended path: retain the wealth plan as the constitution, promote Swing V1 as the only primary strategy, and treat the 15-minute NSE confluence system as a ring-fenced laboratory. Build a shared deterministic core and journal, use OpenAlgo as the initial broker/execution boundary, and postpone infrastructure complexity until measured load or audit requirements justify it.

Keep

Evidence gates, salary firewall, staged capital, cash-equity-first policy, broker-held stops, static-IP deployment, reconciliation, weekly human review and explicit kill criteria.

Reconcile

Swing-first versus intraday-first, direct FYERS versus OpenAlgo, SQLite versus PostgreSQL, and one universal risk percentage versus stage-specific rupee limits.

Remove for now

Local LLM routing, custom six-screen UI, Redis, TimescaleDB, Rust, microservices, options, news sentiment and any AI authority over entries, size or approval.

Important: neither analysis proves an edge. The merged plan is a disciplined method to discover whether one exists. No live date, expected return or capital scale is guaranteed.

What the merge resolves

Reason

Automation removes chart watching, not accountability. Meeting, incident, travel, illness or poor sleep blocks new live entries while existing protection stays active.

Portfolio invariants

  • No transfer from long-term investments, emergency reserves, family assets or Dentics to repair trading losses.
  • Monthly deposits increase reserve capital, not permission to scale.
  • No naked options, expiry speculation, martingale, averaging down or unlimited-risk structures.
  • Trading cannot influence a career reduction decision until three years of reconciled after-tax evidence exists.

Capital hierarchy

  1. Household safety and insurance.
  2. Debt service and free cash buffer.
  3. Long-term diversified equity.
  4. Dentics / SaaS growth.
  5. Trading reserve; only gated slices become deployed capital.

Turn each idea into a strategy contract

The most important missing artifact is not code—it is a frozen, testable rule specification. No implementation begins until ambiguous words become deterministic functions.

1 · UniversePoint-in-time membership, liquidity, price, corporate-action and exclusion rules.
2 · SetupExact support/resistance and regime definitions; no visual discretion.
3 · TriggerClosed-candle entry conditions, timestamp, order type and expiry.
4 · RiskInitial stop, gap policy, quantity rounding and aggregate exposure.
5 · ExitTarget, trailing rule, time stop, end-of-day handling and conflicts.
6 · RecordInputs, rule results, config hash, orders, fills, costs and outcome.

Swing V1 freeze checklist

  • Mechanical S/R and the proposed 50/55/60-day neighbourhood.
  • Point-in-time universe and adjusted-price policy.
  • Entry timing and gap-through-entry behaviour.
  • Stop, target, time exit and rebalance cadence.
  • Maximum sector, symbol and portfolio exposure.
  • Benchmark and strategy-specific acceptance thresholds.

Intraday V1 freeze checklist

  • Write all five confluence conditions mathematically.
  • Define the support/resistance lookback without hindsight.
  • Evaluate only the latest fully closed 15-minute candle.
  • Specify stale/missing candle rejection and duplicate prevention.
  • Specify slippage, partial-fill, rejected-order and square-off rules.
  • Define candidate selection before the session begins.
No silent tuning: a changed threshold creates a new strategy version. Backtest and live execution must import the same rule package and config hash.

Lean shared platform, isolated strategies

Two applications on one Mumbai cloud host: pinned OpenAlgo for broker plumbing and a Python strategy service for the edge. A narrow adapter keeps the broker boundary replaceable.

Operational store

SQLite in WAL mode with migrations and backups. One writer, explicit transactions and unique idempotency keys. Upgrade only against predefined triggers.

Research store

Immutable raw files plus versioned Parquet and DuckDB queries. Preserve point-in-time inputs, adjustment lineage and dataset hashes.

Deployment

One EC2 instance with Elastic IP, Tailscale, encrypted disk, systemd restart and an external heartbeat. Broker-side protective orders outlive App B.

Human interface

Telegram for digest, alert, pause, resume and confirmed kill. OpenAlgo for positions, orders and charts. Add a journal page only when review friction proves the need.

AI boundary

AI summarizes the weekly pack and drafts hypotheses. It cannot generate live orders, change quantity, widen stops, approve itself or deploy config.

Migration triggers

Move to PostgreSQL only for multiple writers/users, audit/query pain, recurring lock contention, or tested recovery needs SQLite cannot meet.

Minimum journal schema

strategy_version, config_hash, dataset_version, symbol, decision timestamp, candle timestamp, raw feature values, every rule result, signal disposition and reason, risk snapshot, client order key, broker IDs, order transitions, fills, fees, taxes, slippage, excursions, exit reason and reconciled outcome. Store skipped signals as carefully as taken trades.

A promotion gate, not a promising chart

Each layer tests a different failure mode. Passing one never waives another, and the final threshold must be frozen before reading the holdout result.

Evidence chain
1 · UnitRule fixtures, boundaries, corporate actions, gaps and sizing arithmetic.
2 · HistoricalWalk-forward, costs, point-in-time universe and parameter neighbours.
3 · ReplayEvent order, closed-candle discipline, duplicate and late-data handling.
4 · ShadowProduction data and schedules; no money; compare expected orders.
5 · Micro-liveReal fills and behaviour with bounded consequence; reconcile daily.

Statistical gate

  • Positive after all estimated brokerage, taxes, fees and conservative slippage.
  • Out-of-sample result meets a predeclared benchmark and drawdown criterion.
  • No single symbol, year or regime dominates the result.
  • Neighbouring parameters remain viable; no needle-peak optimum.
  • Report trade count, exposure, turnover, drawdown duration and uncertainty—not CAGR alone.

Production gate

  • ≥95% rule adherence, with every exception classified.
  • Order idempotency, partial fill, rejection, restart and reconciliation drills pass.
  • Observed slippage and costs remain inside the model's stress envelope.
  • Broker-side stops are visibly active and recovery is tested.
  • Time burden fits the 4–5 hour weekly operating budget.
Anti-overfitting protocol: log hypotheses before tests, keep a final untouched holdout, count all attempted variants, and reject retrospective stories unsupported by a new out-of-sample period.

Slow learning, explicit authority

The system may generate evidence quickly; governance should change slowly. Facts, hypotheses and approvals are separate records.

JournalFreeze the week's trades, skips, incidents, costs and configs.
ScoreCompute expectancy, drawdown, adherence and execution deltas.
ReviewAI drafts observations with model and date clearly labeled.
DecideHuman accepts, rejects or requests more evidence per proposal.
ValidateAccepted hypothesis gets a new version and full test protocol.
DeployOnly a signed, passing config enters the next eligible session.

Human may

Approve, reject, reduce quantity, tighten risk, pause, kill and exit an emergency. All actions are journaled.

Human may not

Increase generated quantity, widen a stop, average down, inject an ungenerated trade, erase a skip, rewrite history or let AI approve its own proposal.

Decisions to close before coding

The source material intentionally leaves some numbers as placeholders. Treat these as work items, not implied recommendations.

  • Freeze all five intraday confluence rules.
  • Select and license point-in-time historical data.
  • Define corporate action, survivorship and missing-data policies.
  • Predeclare statistical promotion and termination thresholds.

Broker and platform

  • Verify current FYERS static-IP, daily login and API rules.
  • Confirm broker-visible protective stops through sandbox/live-minimum tests.
  • Confirm OpenAlgo order tags, RMS applicability and history depth.
  • Measure combined OpenAlgo/App B memory before sizing EC2.
  • Test Tailscale phone login and external outage alerts.

Risk and finance

  • Replace intraday 0.5%, 1.5% and 4% placeholders from evidence.
  • Define swing daily/weekly pause and drawdown limits by stage.
  • Reconcile debt-release dates and the 40/30/20/10 allocation quarterly.
  • Keep insurance, emergency reserve and family assets outside trading.
  • Define withdrawal eligibility; none during drawdown.

Legal and tax

  • Revalidate applicable retail-algo rules before live deployment.
  • Obtain CA documentation for delivery/investment and intraday ledgers.
  • Separate long-term, swing, intraday and Dentics accounting.
  • Review advance-tax and eligible-expense requirements.
  • Repeat compliance and tax review annually.
