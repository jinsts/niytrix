Exposure

80% maximum swing exposure · 10% minimum cash · 2% maximum theoretical open risk.

Scaling

After the pilot, add no more than 20–25% during one quarterly review. Percentage risk falls as capital grows.

Recovery

Root cause, correction, four new shadow weeks and micro-risk restart. Never restart at the previous size.

Operating reference

Checks, evidence and sources

Rules and tax treatment must be revalidated before live deployment and during every annual review.

Before any live swing order
  • Point-in-time universe and adjusted prices validated.
  • After-cost out-of-sample result meets the benchmark/drawdown gate.
  • Parameter neighbours work; no single stock or year dominates.
  • 12 weeks of live-data shadow history completed.
  • FYERS static IP, daily 2FA, order idempotency and reconciliation tested.
  • CA has documented the intended delivery-trade classification.
Human authority

Approve, reject, reduce quantity, tighten risk, pause the system and exit emergencies. Never increase generated quantity, widen stops, average down, inject an ungenerated live trade or let AI approve itself.

Withdrawal policy after year 10

Withdraw only when performance and capital permit. The annual ceiling is the lower of 3% of the trading corpus or 25–35% of the preceding three-year average trading profit. No withdrawals during a drawdown.

What would make the project a success even if trading stops?

A tested data platform, stronger quantitative research ability, disciplined capital allocation, a reliable tax ledger, clearer behaviour under uncertainty and an evidence-based decision to redirect capital are all valid outcomes.
